# Preferred Coaching Sources

These sources carry higher credibility weight when evaluating WebSearch results for `research-methodology`. Prefer content from these authors and platforms when multiple results are available.

## Primary Evidence-Based Sources

### Books & Authors
- **Jack Daniels** — *Daniels' Running Formula* (VDOT, pace zones, periodization)
- **Pete Pfitzinger** — *Advanced Marathoning* (marathon-specific training, peaking)
- **Steve Magness** — *The Science of Running* (modern physiology, performance)
- **Matt Fitzgerald** — *80/20 Running* (polarized training, intensity distribution)
- **Stephen Seiler** — Polarized training research (80/20 intensity split, low-intensity dominance)

### Research Journals (prefer peer-reviewed)
- *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance (IJSPP)*
- *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise (MSSE)*
- *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*
- *European Journal of Applied Physiology*

### Trusted Web Sources
- **PubMed** (pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) — primary literature
- **Runner's World** (runnersworld.com) — practical application, interview-based
- **Alex Hutchinson / Sweat Science** (outsideonline.com/sweatscience) — evidence-based analysis
- **Floris Gierman** (extramilest.com) — MAF/heart rate training, evidence summaries
- **Alan Couzens** (alancouzens.com) — training load and physiology analysis
- **Kofuzi YouTube** — training log insights (use for qualitative context only)

## Topics With Strong Evidence Base

The following topics have well-established protocols — cite primary sources directly rather than opinion pieces:

- VDOT and race pace prediction → Daniels
- Polarized 80/20 intensity split → Seiler (numerous papers)
- Heat acclimation → ~10-14 days; plasma volume expansion → Périard et al. (IJSPP)
- Altitude preparation → 3+ weeks at 2000-2400m or live-high/train-low → Chapman et al.
- Post-illness return → "above-the-neck rule" (widely cited, limited RCT evidence)
- Taper response → 2-3 week taper, volume -40 to -60% → Bosquet et al. (Medicine & Science)

## Topics to Handle with Care

- **Injury rehab protocols** — recommend physio consultation; do not prescribe specific exercises
- **Nutrition and fueling** — out of scope for this plugin; defer to a nutrition specialist
- **Supplement recommendations** — high variability; cite only WADA-safe, evidence-backed compounds if asked
